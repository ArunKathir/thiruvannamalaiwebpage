Privacy Policy

How Tiruvannamalai Rooms handles your personal information.

Last updated: September 2026

Who We Are

Tiruvannamalai Rooms ("we", "us", "our") is an accommodation booking agency operating through the website tiruvannamalairooms.com. We help visitors find accommodation in Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, India.

Information We Collect

When you use our service, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name (if provided)
  • Phone number (via WhatsApp communication)
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Travel dates and accommodation preferences
  • Budget and location preferences
  • Any additional requirements you share with us

Our website does not require account creation or login. The primary communication channel is WhatsApp.

How We Use Your Information

  • To search for and recommend suitable accommodation based on your requirements
  • To facilitate bookings with accommodation properties
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry or booking
  • To improve our service

Information Sharing

We share your travel requirements (dates, number of guests, preferences) with accommodation properties when checking availability and making bookings on your behalf. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your WhatsApp messages are stored on WhatsApp's platform according to their privacy policy. We do not maintain a separate customer database beyond our WhatsApp conversation history and any booking records we maintain for service purposes.

Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies unless explicitly disclosed.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us via WhatsApp or email. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
