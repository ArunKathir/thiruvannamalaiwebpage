Hotels in Tiruvannamalai What to Expect

Tiruvannamalai has a wide range of hotels, from budget lodges to mid-range and premium properties. The town primarily caters to temple visitors and pilgrims, but the hotel landscape has been evolving to serve families, couples and business travellers as well.

Most hotels in Tiruvannamalai are clustered around the Arunachaleswarar Temple area, along the main roads leading to the bus stand, and near the Girivalam route. A smaller number of properties can be found near Ramana Ashram (Sri Ramanasramam), which tends to be a quieter part of town.

Types of Hotels Available

  • Budget Hotels Basic rooms, often fan-only or with AC options. Typically under ₹1,500 per night. Good for short pilgrim stays.
  • Mid-Range Hotels AC rooms with attached bathrooms, hot water, Wi-Fi, and sometimes a restaurant. ₹1,500-₹3,000 per night.
  • Premium Hotels Well-appointed rooms with modern amenities, better furnishings, room service, and often parking. ₹3,000+ per night.
  • Lodge-Style Accommodation Simple, no-frills rooms popular with pilgrims during festival seasons. Very affordable.

Difference Between Hotels and Rooms

In Tiruvannamalai, "hotels" typically refers to more established properties with reception desks, multiple rooms, and sometimes restaurants. "Rooms" can include guesthouses, serviced apartments, lodges, and smaller properties. We help you find both types based on your needs.
