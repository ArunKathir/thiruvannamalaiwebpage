Staying Near Ramana Ashram

Sri Ramanasramam commonly known as Ramana Ashram is located at the foot of Arunachala hill in Tiruvannamalai. It is the ashram founded in honour of Sri Ramana Maharshi, one of India's most revered spiritual teachers. Thousands of visitors from India and around the world visit the ashram throughout the year.

The area around Ramana Ashram is notably quieter and more peaceful compared to the busy temple area in the town centre. This makes it a popular choice for visitors seeking a tranquil stay, longer retreats, or a more contemplative atmosphere.

What to Expect Near Ramana Ashram

  • Quiet environment Significantly less noise and traffic compared to the temple area.
  • Proximity to Arunachala The ashram sits at the base of the hill, offering views and easy access to walking paths.
  • Spiritual atmosphere Many ashram visitors, meditation centres, and yoga retreats are in this area.

  • Moderate distance from the temple Arunachaleswarar Temple is approximately 2-3 kilometres away, accessible by auto-rickshaw in about 10 minutes.

Who Should Stay Near Ramana Ashram?

  • Visitors to Sri Ramanasramam
  • Spiritual seekers and meditation practitioners
  • Visitors who prefer a quieter, more peaceful environment
  • Long-stay visitors (those staying a week or more)
  • Elderly visitors who prefer calm surroundings
  • International visitors familiar with ashram culture

Temple Visitors

If your primary purpose is daily temple darshan and you want to be within walking distance of Arunachaleswarar Temple, consider rooms near the temple instead. The temple area is the more convenient option for frequent temple visits.
