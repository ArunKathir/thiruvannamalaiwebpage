Tiruvannamalai Accommodation Guide

Everything you need to know about finding and booking accommodation in Tiruvannamalai.

This guide is designed to help first-time and repeat visitors to Tiruvannamalai make informed decisions about where to stay. Whether you are coming for a temple visit, Girivalam, Ramana Ashram, or a family trip this guide covers the practical information you need.

Last updated: September 2026

About Tiruvannamalai

Tiruvannamalai is a temple town in Tamil Nadu, India, located at the foot of the sacred Arunachala hill. It is home to the ancient Arunachaleswarar Temple (one of the Pancha Bhoota Sthalas), Sri Ramanasramam (Ramana Ashram), and the 14-km Girivalam path around Arunachala. The town attracts millions of visitors annually.

Types of Accommodation Available

  • Hotels Established properties with reception, multiple room types, and sometimes restaurants. See hotels
  • Guesthouses Smaller properties, often family-run, with a more personal feel
  • Lodges Basic, affordable rooms popular with pilgrims
  • Serviced rooms Independently maintained rooms or apartments

Budget Guide

  • Budget (under ₹1,500) Clean basic rooms, fan or AC
  • Mid-range (₹1,500-₹3,000) Good AC rooms with modern bathrooms
  • Premium (₹3,000+) Best amenities, comfort, and service

When to Visit

  • October-February Best weather. Pleasant temperatures. Peak pilgrimage season. Book early.
  • March-June Hot season. AC rooms strongly recommended. Lower demand means more availability.
  • July-September Monsoon season. Occasional rains. Moderate temperatures.
  • Full moon days Very high demand for Girivalam. Book at least 1-2 weeks in advance.
  • Deepam festival (November/December) The biggest event. Rooms book out weeks in advance.

How to Get to Tiruvannamalai

  • From Chennai Approximately 190 km, 3.5-4 hours by road. Buses available from CMBT and Koyambedu.
  • From Bengaluru Approximately 200 km, 4-4.5 hours by road.
  • From Pondicherry Approximately 105 km, 2.5 hours by road.
  • By train Tiruvannamalai has a railway station connected via Villupuram junction.
  • Nearest airport Chennai International Airport (approximately 190 km).

Booking Tips

  • Book well in advance for festival periods, full moon weekends, and peak season (October-January).
  • Confirm check-in and check-out times, especially if arriving late or leaving early.
  • Verify amenities (AC, hot water, parking) before booking rather than assuming.
  • For stays longer than 3 days, ask about multi-night discounts.
  • If you have specific needs (ground floor, quiet room, early check-in), communicate them clearly.
