Rooms by Location

Location matters greatly in Tiruvannamalai. Depending on what you are visiting for, different areas suit different needs:

  • Near Arunachaleswarar Temple Best for pilgrims and devotees who want walking-distance access to the temple. This is the most popular area for accommodation.
  • Near Ramana Ashram Quieter surroundings, ideal for those visiting Sri Ramanasramam and seeking a peaceful atmosphere.
  • Near the Girivalam Route Convenient for visitors doing the sacred 14-kilometre circumambulation of Arunachala hill, especially during full moon.
  • Near the Bus Stand / Railway Station Practical for travellers arriving by bus or train who prefer proximity to transport hubs.

What to Look for When Booking a Room

When searching for accommodation in Tiruvannamalai, consider the following:

  • Air conditioning Tiruvannamalai can be very hot, especially from March to June. AC rooms provide much-needed comfort.
  • Hot water Essential for early morning temple visits. Check whether hot water is available 24 hours or only during certain hours.
  • Parking If you are driving from Chennai or Bengaluru, on-site parking is a significant convenience.
  • Cleanliness and maintenance We prioritise this in our recommendations. Freshly maintained rooms make a real difference.
  • Proximity to your destination Walking distance to the temple versus a short auto-rickshaw ride can affect your entire experience.
