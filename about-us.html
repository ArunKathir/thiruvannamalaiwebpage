About Tiruvannamalai Rooms

A local accommodation booking agency focused on Tiruvannamalai.

Who We Are

Tiruvannamalai Rooms is a local accommodation booking agency based in Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, India. We help visitors find suitable places to stay in Tiruvannamalai based on their budget, preferred location, and specific requirements.

We Are Not a Hotel

We want to be clear: we do not own or operate any hotels or accommodation properties. We are a booking agency and concierge service. When you book through us, you stay at the recommended property. We act as the trusted intermediary who helps you find the right place.

What We Do

Our core service is simple:

  1. You tell us your travel dates, budget, preferred area (near the temple, near Ramana Ashram, near the Girivalam route, etc.), and any special requirements.
  2. We search through our network of properties in Tiruvannamalai that we have personally assessed.
  3. We recommend options that match your needs.
  4. Once you choose, we help facilitate the booking with the property.

Why We Exist

Tiruvannamalai is a major pilgrimage and spiritual destination. Millions of people visit the Arunachaleswarar Temple, Ramana Ashram, and the Girivalam route every year. Yet finding reliable, clean, and appropriately-priced accommodation can be challenging — especially for first-time visitors or those coming from other cities.

Online travel agencies often have limited or outdated listings for smaller towns like Tiruvannamalai. Many good local properties are not listed online at all. And visitors do not always know which area of the town is best suited to their particular needs.

Tiruvannamalai Rooms was created to solve this problem. We combine local knowledge with a personal service to help visitors find the right stay.

Our Approach

  • We visit properties in person. We do not simply aggregate online listings. We personally visit accommodation options in Tiruvannamalai to assess quality, cleanliness, and suitability.
  • We are selective. We do not list every property in town. We focus on stays we believe are worth recommending based on our assessment.
  • We are transparent. We clearly distinguish between what we have verified first-hand and what is property-provided information.
  • We earn commission. We earn a commission from the property when a booking is successfully completed. There is no additional charge to the customer.

How We Make Money

Transparency about our business model is important for trust. We earn a commission from accommodation properties when a booking is completed through our service. The room rate you pay is the rate set by the property — we do not add extra charges on top.
