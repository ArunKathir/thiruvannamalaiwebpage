Hotels Near the Temple

The Arunachaleswarar Temple area is the most popular location for accommodation in Tiruvannamalai. Several hotels are located within 500 metres to 1 kilometre of the main temple entrance, offering easy access for morning and evening darshan.

Hotels in this area range from simple pilgrim lodges to well-appointed properties with AC rooms, restaurants, and modern amenities.

Advantages of Staying Near the Temple

  • Walking distance to morning and evening darshan
  • Close to temple festivals and events
  • Surrounded by shops, flower markets, and vegetarian restaurants
  • Central location with good auto-rickshaw and bus connectivity
  • Largest selection of accommodation options in town

Things to Consider

  • Noise levels The temple area is the busiest part of Tiruvannamalai. Expect sounds from the temple, shops, and traffic.
  • Crowd during festivals Full moon days, Deepam, and other festivals draw very large crowds. Book early.
  • Parking Not all temple-area hotels have on-site parking. Verify before booking if you are driving.
