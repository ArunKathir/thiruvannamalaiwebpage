The Complete Guide to Girivalam

Everything you need to know about circumambulating the sacred Arunachala Hill.

What is Girivalam?

Girivalam is the sacred practice of circumambulating (walking around) the holy Arunachala Hill in Tiruvannamalai. The word translates from Tamil: 'Giri' means hill, and 'Valam' means coming around. It is considered one of the most powerful spiritual practices in Shaivism.

The 14km Sacred Route

The total distance of the Girivalam path is approximately 14 kilometers. The route is well-paved and dotted with numerous shrines, ashrams, and the highly revered Ashta Lingams (Eight Lingams).

The Ashta Lingams (Eight Lingams)

During the walk, devotees visit eight distinct Shiva lingams situated at the eight cardinal directions of the hill. Each lingam is associated with a specific astrological sign and planetary deity:

  1. Indra Lingam (East) - Associated with Sun and Venus
  2. Agni Lingam (South East) - Associated with Moon
  3. Yama Lingam (South) - Associated with Mars
  4. Niruthi Lingam (South West) - Associated with Rahu
  5. Varuna Lingam (West) - Associated with Saturn
  6. Vayu Lingam (North West) - Associated with Ketu
  7. Kubera Lingam (North) - Associated with Jupiter
  8. Easanya Lingam (North East) - Associated with Mercury

Rules and Guidelines for Girivalam

Best Practices for Devotees

  • Walk Barefoot: It is highly recommended to walk barefoot to absorb the spiritual energy of the sacred ground.
  • Keep the Hill on your Right: Always walk in a clockwise direction.
  • Dress Modestly: Wear traditional and comfortable cotton clothing.
  • Maintain Silence: Chant the mantra "Om Namah Shivaya" or keep silent. Avoid casual conversations.
  • Hydration: Carry a water bottle, though numerous water stations are available along the path.
