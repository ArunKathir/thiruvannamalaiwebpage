Best Time to Visit Tiruvannamalai

Plan your trip around the best weather, Pournami days, and grand temple festivals.

Understanding Tiruvannamalai's Climate

Tiruvannamalai experiences a tropical climate. Because the city is located in the inland plains of Tamil Nadu, the weather is predominantly hot and dry for most of the year. To ensure a comfortable spiritual journey, especially if you plan to walk the 14km Girivalam path barefoot, choosing the right time is crucial.

The Ideal Season: Winter (October to March)

This is undeniably the best time to visit Tiruvannamalai.

  • Weather: The temperature drops to a comfortable 20°C to 28°C. The mornings are pleasantly cool, and the evenings are perfect for meditation and circumambulation.
  • Festivals: This season encompasses major festivals including Navaratri, Deepavali, and the grandest of all—Karthigai Deepam (usually late November or early December).
  • Vibe: The town is vibrant, and the energy around Arunachala is at its peak with thousands of seekers arriving for the winter retreat season.

The Shoulder Season: Monsoon (July to September)

If you prefer fewer crowds and a greener landscape, the monsoon season is an excellent alternative.

  • Weather: Tiruvannamalai receives moderate rainfall. The showers bring down the intense summer heat, leaving the Arunachala Hill lush and green.
  • Tip: Carry an umbrella or raincoat. Walking the Girivalam path during a light drizzle can be a profoundly beautiful and cooling experience.

The Challenging Season: Summer (April to June)

Summer in Tiruvannamalai can be intense and is generally recommended only for those accustomed to high heat.

  • Weather: Temperatures regularly soar between 35°C and 40°C+. The sun beats down intensely on the rocky Arunachala Hill.
  • Advice: If you must visit during summer, restrict all outdoor activities, including temple visits and Girivalam, to early morning (before 8 AM) or late evening (after 6 PM).

Planning Around Pournami (Full Moon)

Regardless of the season, the most powerful and popular time to visit is during the Full Moon (Pournami) every month. Over a million devotees descend on the town to perform Girivalam. If you plan to visit during Pournami, booking your accommodation months in advance is absolutely essential.
