Visitor Guide: Sri Ramana Ashram

Experiencing peace and profound silence at the abode of Bhagavan Sri Ramana Maharshi.

Introduction to the Ashram

Located at the foothills of the holy Arunachala Hill, Sri Ramana Ashram is where the great sage Sri Ramana Maharshi lived for 54 years until his Mahanirvana in 1950. Today, it remains a vibrant center of spiritual practice, drawing seekers from across the globe who come to experience the profound silence and presence that still permeates the grounds.

Ashram Timings & Daily Schedule

The ashram follows a structured daily routine. While visitors can sit in silence almost anytime during open hours, there are specific poojas and Veda Parayanam (chanting) sessions that are highly recommended to attend.

General Timings

  • Morning Hours: 5:00 AM to 12:00 PM
  • Afternoon/Evening Hours: 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM

Key Daily Events

  • 6:45 AM: Milk Offering to Sri Bhagavan
  • 8:00 AM: Breakfast (for ashram inmates and guests)
  • 8:30 AM to 9:15 AM: Veda Parayanam (Vedic chanting) at Bhagavan's Samadhi
  • 11:30 AM: Lunch
  • 5:00 PM to 5:45 PM: Evening Veda Parayanam
  • 6:45 PM: Tamil Chanting (Parayanam)

What to See Inside the Ashram

The ashram complex has several places of significance:

  • The Samadhi Hall: The focal point of the ashram, housing the tomb of Sri Ramana Maharshi.
  • The Old Hall: The room where Bhagavan sat for over two decades, giving darshan to devotees. It is incredibly potent for meditation.
  • Matrubhuteswara Temple: The temple built over the samadhi of Bhagavan's mother.
  • The Library: Contains a vast collection of spiritual literature in various languages.
  • Peacocks and Animals: True to Bhagavan's love for animals, you will find beautiful peacocks roaming freely.

Rules & Dress Code

To maintain the sanctity of the ashram, visitors are expected to adhere to the following guidelines:

  • Dress Code: Shoulders and knees must be covered. Men should wear full trousers and shirts/kurtas. Women should wear sarees, salwar kameez, or long skirts. Shorts, sleeveless tops, and tight clothing are strictly prohibited.
  • Silence: Maintain absolute silence in the meditation halls.
  • Photography: Photography inside the Old Hall and Samadhi Hall is restricted. Please look for signage.
