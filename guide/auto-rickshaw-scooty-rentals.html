Auto Rickshaws & Scooty Rentals

How to get around Tiruvannamalai easily and fairly.

While Tiruvannamalai is largely walkable, especially if you stay near Ramana Ashram or the Temple, you will inevitably need transport to visit remote ashrams or the bus stand. Here is how to navigate the town's transport options without overpaying.

1. Auto Rickshaws

Auto rickshaws (tuk-tuks) are everywhere. However, they rarely use meters in Tiruvannamalai.

  • Negotiate First: Always agree on the fare before getting into the auto.
  • Standard Fares: A short trip (e.g., Ramana Ashram to the Temple) should cost around ₹80 - ₹100. Traveling from the bus stand to the ashram area may cost ₹150.
  • Girivalam by Auto: If you cannot walk the 14km path, you can hire an auto to drive you around the mountain, stopping at the 8 lingams. This usually costs between ₹600 to ₹800.

2. Scooty Rentals (Two-Wheelers)

Renting a scooter is the most popular option for long-term visitors and digital nomads. It gives you complete freedom to explore the countryside and visit organic cafes on the outskirts of town.

  • Where to Rent: Several rental shops are located on the main road opposite Sri Ramanasramam (Chengham Road).
  • Cost: Expect to pay between ₹300 to ₹400 per day. For a monthly rental, you can negotiate it down to ₹4000 - ₹5000.
  • Requirements: You will need to provide a copy of your passport/ID and sometimes a small cash deposit. A valid driving license is required by law.
