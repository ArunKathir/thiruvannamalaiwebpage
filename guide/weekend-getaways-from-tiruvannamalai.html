Weekend Getaways from Tiruvannamalai

The best nearby destinations for nature, history, and beaches.

While Tiruvannamalai itself is a powerful spiritual magnet, its central location in Tamil Nadu makes it a fantastic base camp for exploring the wider region. If you are staying long-term and need a change of scenery, here are the top weekend getaways.

1. Pondicherry & Auroville (110 km)

A quick 2.5-hour drive takes you from the deep Hindu traditions of Arunachala to the breezy French colonial streets of Pondicherry.

  • What to do: Walk the Promenade beach, eat authentic French pastries, and visit Auroville—the experimental international township dedicated to human unity.
  • Vibe: Coastal, cosmopolitan, and relaxed.

2. Gingee Fort (40 km)

Known as the "Troy of the East," Gingee Fort is one of the most impregnable fortresses in India, spread across three massive hillocks.

  • What to do: Hike to the top of Rajagiri (the main fort). It is a steep, rewarding climb that takes about an hour.
  • Tip: Go very early in the morning. By 10 AM, the heat reflecting off the rocks becomes unbearable.

3. Yelagiri Hills (120 km)

If you need to escape the heat of the plains, Yelagiri is a small, quiet hill station.

  • What to do: Enjoy boating on Punganur Lake, go for short nature walks, and enjoy the cool, misty climate.
  • Vibe: Quiet, family-friendly, and very laid back.
