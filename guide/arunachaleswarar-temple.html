Visiting the Arunachaleswarar Temple

Navigate one of the largest temple complexes in India. Learn about darshan timings, architecture, and rituals.

The Arunachaleswarar Temple (also known as Annamalaiyar Temple) in Tiruvannamalai is one of the most significant Shaivite temples in the world. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, it is one of the Pancha Bhoota Stalas, representing the element of Fire (Agni). Located at the base of the Arunachala hill, this sprawling 25-acre complex is a masterpiece of Dravidian architecture.

Architecture and Layout

The temple is renowned for its massive gopurams (gateway towers). The eastern tower, the Rajagopuram, is one of the tallest in India, standing at 217 feet (66 meters) with 11 stories. As you walk through the complex, you will pass through five concentric courtyards (prakarams), each featuring stunning mandapams (pillared halls), smaller shrines, and sacred tanks.

Key architectural highlights include:

  • The Thousand Pillared Hall: A magnificent structure built during the Vijayanagara period.
  • The Pathala Lingam: An underground chamber where Sri Ramana Maharshi meditated in deep absorption shortly after arriving in Tiruvannamalai.
  • Shivaganga Tank: The sacred water tank where devotees purify themselves.

Darshan Timings

General Timings:

  • Morning: 5:00 AM to 12:30 PM
  • Evening: 3:30 PM to 9:30 PM

* Note: Timings may vary slightly during major festivals like Karthigai Deepam or Pournami (Full Moon days).

Tips for Visitors

Visiting such a massive temple can be overwhelming, especially on busy days. Here are some tips to ensure a smooth experience:

  • Free vs. Special Darshan: There is a free queue (Dharma Darshan) which can take several hours on busy days. You can also purchase Special Darshan tickets (usually ₹50 or ₹200 depending on the queue length) to expedite your entry.
  • Dress Code: Traditional Indian wear is required. Men should wear dhoti or formal pants (no shorts). Women should wear sarees, half-sarees, or churidars with a dupatta.
  • Leave Electronics Behind: Mobile phones and cameras are generally not permitted inside the inner sanctum. It is best to leave them in your hotel room.

Accommodation Near the Temple

If your primary focus is visiting the temple multiple times or attending early morning pujas, staying near the Arunachaleswarar Temple is highly convenient. The area is bustling with activity, local eateries, and easy access to auto-rickshaws.
