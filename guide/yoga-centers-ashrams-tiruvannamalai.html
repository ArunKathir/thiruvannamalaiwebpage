Yoga Centers & Ashrams

Where to practice Hatha, Ashtanga, and spiritual Yoga.

Tiruvannamalai is not just about meditation; it is also a growing hub for classical Yoga. Whether you are a beginner looking for drop-in classes or a dedicated practitioner seeking a long-term ashram experience, the town offers excellent options.

The Yoga Scene

Most yoga centers are concentrated around the Ramana Ashram area (Ramana Nagar). During the peak season (November to March), international teachers host workshops, TTC (Teacher Training Courses), and daily drop-in classes.

Top Places to Practice

  • Ramana Ashram (Self-Inquiry): While they don't teach physical asanas, this is the ultimate place for Jnana Yoga (the yoga of knowledge) and meditation.
  • Seshadri Ashram: Offers a peaceful environment with occasional spiritual discourses and quiet halls for personal practice.
  • Siva Marga Yoga: A popular center near the Girivalam path offering daily Hatha and Vinyasa drop-in classes.
  • Satya Yoga Shala: Known for strict, traditional Ashtanga Vinyasa methodology.

Choosing the Right Practice

In Tiruvannamalai, the focus often shifts from purely physical fitness to spiritual awakening. Many practitioners combine morning physical Asana practice with afternoon meditation inside the ashrams or on the Arunachala hill to balance the body and mind.
