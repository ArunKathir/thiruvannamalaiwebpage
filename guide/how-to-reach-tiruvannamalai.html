How to Reach Tiruvannamalai

Your comprehensive guide to traveling via air, rail, and road.

Reaching by Air (Flights)

Tiruvannamalai does not have its own airport. The two most convenient options are:

  • Chennai International Airport (MAA) - 185 km away: This is the most common entry point for international and domestic travelers. From Chennai, you can hire a private prepaid taxi directly to Tiruvannamalai, which takes about 3.5 to 4 hours. Alternatively, you can take a bus from Chennai's CMBT (Koyambedu) bus terminus.
  • Bengaluru International Airport (BLR) - 220 km away: The drive from Bengaluru airport takes about 4.5 to 5 hours. It's a smooth drive, often preferred by travelers coming from the West or North of India.

Reaching by Train (Railways)

Tiruvannamalai Railway Station (Station Code: TNM) is located right in the heart of the town, about 2 kilometers from the Arunachaleswarar Temple.

  • It falls on the Villupuram-Katpadi branch line.
  • There are direct trains from Chennai, Bengaluru, Madurai, and Trichy, though frequency may be limited.
  • Pro Tip: If you cannot find a direct train to TNM, book a ticket to Villupuram Junction (VM) (65 km away) or Katpadi Junction (KPD) (90 km away). Both are major railway hubs. From there, you can easily catch a connecting passenger train, bus, or taxi to Tiruvannamalai.

Reaching by Road (Buses & Driving)

Tiruvannamalai is exceptionally well-connected by road, maintained by the TNSTC (Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation) and private operators.

Key Bus Routes

  • From Chennai: Frequent AC and non-AC buses depart from Kilambakkam (KCBT) and Koyambedu (CMBT). The journey takes approximately 4 hours.
  • From Bengaluru: Direct overnight sleeper buses and daytime seater buses are available from Shantinagar and Madiwala. The journey takes about 4.5 to 5 hours.
  • From Pondicherry: Just 110 km away, buses run frequently, taking about 2.5 hours.

Local Transport in Tiruvannamalai

Once you arrive, getting around is easy. Auto-rickshaws are the primary mode of local transport. They are available at the railway station and bus stand. Be sure to negotiate the fare before starting your trip, or use shared autos that ply the main Girivalam road for a fixed minimal rate.
