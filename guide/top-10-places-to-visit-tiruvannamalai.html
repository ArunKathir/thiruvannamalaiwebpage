Top 10 Places to Visit in Tiruvannamalai

The ultimate tourist guide to the most spiritual and scenic locations in town.

Tiruvannamalai is one of the most revered spiritual destinations in India. While most people know it for the famous Arunachaleswarar Temple and the Girivalam path, there are many other incredible places to explore. Here is our curated list of the top 10 places to visit.

1. Arunachaleswarar Temple

The heart of Tiruvannamalai. This magnificent 9th-century temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva, representing the element of fire. Its massive gopurams (towers) dominate the town's skyline.

2. Sri Ramana Ashram

The spiritual home of the great sage Sri Ramana Maharshi. Visitors from all over the world come here to experience the profound silence and practice Self-Inquiry meditation.

3. The Girivalam Path

A 14-kilometer circumambulation path around the holy Arunachala Hill. Even if it is not a full moon day, walking this path early in the morning is a deeply peaceful experience.

4. Skandashram & Virupaksha Cave

Located on the eastern slope of Arunachala Hill, these caves were where Ramana Maharshi meditated for decades. The trek up offers stunning panoramic views of the main temple.

5. Seshadri Swamigal Ashram

Located right next to Ramana Ashram, this peaceful ashram is dedicated to Mahan Sri Seshadri Swamigal. It has beautiful gardens and a highly vibrating samadhi shrine.

6. Yogi Ramsuratkumar Ashram

Dedicated to the "Godchild of Tiruvannamalai", this massive, modern ashram features a beautiful meditation hall and is known for its discipline and chanting.

7. Parvathamalai

Located about 30 km from Tiruvannamalai, this is one of the most thrilling and difficult treks in Tamil Nadu. The hilltop temple dedicated to Lord Mallikarjunaswamy requires climbing steep iron steps and crowbars.

8. Sathanur Dam

A beautiful picnic spot located 30 km away, built across the Thenpennai river. It features lush parks, a massive crocodile farm, and is perfect for a family day trip.

9. Kubera Lingam & Ashta Lingams

While on the Girivalam path, make sure to visit all eight directional Lingams. The Kubera Lingam (North) is particularly famous among devotees seeking prosperity.

10. Gingee Fort

Located about 40 km away on the way to Villupuram, Gingee Fort (known as the Troy of the East) is a massive, impregnable hill fort with incredible historical significance.
