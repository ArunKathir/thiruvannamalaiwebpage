Where to Stay in Tiruvannamalai

A practical area-by-area guide to help you choose the right location for your stay in Tiruvannamalai.

Choosing where to stay in Tiruvannamalai depends on your purpose of visit, budget, and personal preferences. The town has several distinct areas, each offering a different experience. This guide will help you decide which area is right for you.

Near Arunachaleswarar Temple

Best for: Pilgrims, temple devotees, first-time visitors, short stays

The area around the Arunachaleswarar Temple is the busiest and most central part of Tiruvannamalai. It offers the widest range of accommodation options from very basic pilgrim lodges to mid-range hotels. Walking-distance access to the temple is the primary advantage.

Pros: Closest to the temple, many food options, easy access to shops and services.
Cons: Noisy, crowded during festivals, limited parking.

Near Ramana Ashram

Best for: Ashram visitors, spiritual seekers, long-stay visitors, elderly travellers

The Ramana Ashram area is at the foot of Arunachala hill, about 2-3 km from the temple. It is notably quieter and more peaceful. Several guesthouses and small hotels cater to ashram visitors, including many international travellers.

Pros: Peaceful, close to Arunachala, spiritual atmosphere, cafÃ©s with diverse cuisines.
Cons: Fewer accommodation options, further from the temple and bus stand.

Near the Girivalam Route

Best for: Girivalam visitors, full moon pilgrims

Some properties are located along or near the 14-km Girivalam path around Arunachala. This can be convenient for those who want to start or end the circumambulation near their accommodation.

Pros: Direct access to the Girivalam path.
Cons: May be far from the town centre and temple for other activities.

Town Centre / Near Bus Stand

Best for: Transit travellers, those arriving by bus, budget visitors

The area near the Tiruvannamalai bus stand and main market is practical for visitors arriving by bus from Chennai, Bengaluru, Pondicherry or other towns. Budget accommodation is readily available.

Pros: Close to transport, affordable, central location.
Cons: Commercial area, not as close to the temple as the temple area itself.

Near the Railway Station

Best for: Visitors arriving by train

The Tiruvannamalai railway station area has some accommodation options. The station is connected by trains from Villupuram junction, which links to Chennai and other cities.

Summary: Which Area Should You Choose?

Temple devotee? → Near Arunachaleswarar Temple
Ashram visitor? → Near Ramana Ashram
Girivalam? → Near the Girivalam route or temple area
Family with elderly? → Near Ramana Ashram (quieter) or good family hotel near the temple
Budget traveller? → Budget rooms near the bus stand or temple area
