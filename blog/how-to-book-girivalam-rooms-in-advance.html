How to Book Rooms for Girivalam in Advance Without Hassle

Don't get stranded! The ultimate strategy for securing the best rooms during Pournami.

Every full moon (Pournami), over a million devotees descend upon Tiruvannamalai to perform Girivalam (circumambulating the holy Arunachala hill). With such a massive influx of visitors, finding a clean, affordable, and well-located room becomes a significant challenge.

If you plan to visit during Pournami, walking into a hotel without a reservation is risky. You will likely face sold-out signs, extreme price surges (sometimes 3x the normal rate), or subpar accommodation. Here is our comprehensive guide to booking your Girivalam room in advance without the stress.

1. Know the Exact Pournami Dates

The very first step is to know the exact dates. Pournami falls on a different date each month according to the lunar calendar. More importantly, the "auspicious time" for walking usually spans across two calendar days.

You can check our Updated Pournami Girivalam Dates page. Always plan to book your room for the night when the peak auspicious time occurs.

2. How Early Should You Book?

  • For Regular Pournami: Book at least 3 to 4 weeks in advance. The best properties near the temple or Ramanasramam fill up a month prior.
  • For Chitra Pournami (April/May): This is the biggest full moon of the year. Book at least 2 months in advance.
  • For Karthigai Deepam (Nov/Dec): While not technically just a Girivalam event, this is the busiest festival. You must book 3 to 4 months in advance.

3. Choosing the Right Location

Where you stay impacts your Girivalam experience heavily. You have three main options:

A. Near the Main Temple (Arunachaleswarar Temple)

Pros: You are right at the starting point of the traditional Girivalam route. Easy access for temple darshan.

Cons: Extremely crowded, noisy, and traffic gets blocked during Pournami.

B. Near Ramana Ashram / Chengam Road

Pros: A more peaceful atmosphere, great cafes, and many western/premium guesthouses. It's on the Girivalam path, so you can just step out and start walking.

Cons: Slightly more expensive. It's about 2km away from the main temple entrance.

C. On the Bypass / Outskirts

Pros: Cheaper rates, easier parking if you are driving, and less chaotic.

Cons: You will have to walk a fair bit just to get to the Girivalam path, and finding auto-rickshaws during Pournami is difficult.

4. Beware of the "Pournami Price Surge"

It is a known fact that many hotels inflate their prices dramatically 24-48 hours before the full moon. A room that normally costs ₹1,500 might suddenly be quoted at ₹4,000.

The Solution: Booking online in advance locks in your rate. Avoid calling a hotel on the day of your arrival, as you will be subjected to surge pricing. By using a trusted booking platform, you guarantee your price.

5. What to Look For When Booking

  • 24/7 Hot Water: After a 14km walk, a hot shower is essential. Ensure the property guarantees this.
  • Parking: If driving, confirm parking is available inside the property. Streets get blocked off during Pournami.
  • AC vs Non-AC: During summer (March-August), AC is a must. In winter (Nov-Feb), Non-AC is perfectly fine and saves money.
