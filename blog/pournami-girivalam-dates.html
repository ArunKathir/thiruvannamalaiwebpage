Pournami Girivalam Dates

The complete schedule for Full Moon circumambulation of the holy Arunachala Hill.

Walking the 14km Girivalam path around Arunachala during Pournami (Full Moon) is considered profoundly auspicious. Millions of devotees gather every month for this sacred circumambulation.

The exact timing of the Full Moon (Pournami Tithi) changes based on the Hindu lunar calendar. It does not always align perfectly with a midnight-to-midnight Gregorian day. Often, the tithi begins on one afternoon and ends the following afternoon.

Important Advice for Pournami Visitors

  • Book Rooms Extremely Early: Hotels and ashrams in Tiruvannamalai get fully booked weeks, sometimes months, before a Pournami date. Do not arrive without a reservation expecting to find a room.
  • Traffic Restrictions: On Pournami days, police restrict vehicle entry into the town. You will have to park in designated lots on the outskirts and walk or take special buses into town.
  • When to Walk: While you can start walking at any time during the Tithi, starting around 3:00 AM or 4:00 AM is recommended to avoid the heaviest crush of the crowd and to enjoy the cool morning air.
