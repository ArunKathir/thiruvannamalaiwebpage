What to Pack for Tiruvannamalai

An essential packing list for temple visits, ashram life, and local weather.

Tiruvannamalai is a town of stark contrasts—scorching hot afternoons, deeply traditional temples, and rocky mountain treks. Packing correctly is the key to a comfortable pilgrimage.

1. Clothing (The Dress Code)

Modesty is required everywhere in town, not just inside the temples and ashrams.

  • Lightweight Cotton: Pack breathable cotton clothes. The heat can be intense.
  • For Men: Long cotton trousers, jeans, or traditional dhotis. T-shirts and collared shirts are fine.
  • For Women: Kurtas with leggings and a dupatta (scarf), long skirts, or conservative dresses that cover shoulders and knees. A lightweight scarf is the most versatile item you can pack.

2. Footwear

You will take your shoes off multiple times a day.

  • Slip-on Sandals: Essential for quickly removing footwear outside temples and restaurants.
  • Walking Shoes: If you plan to hike up to the caves or walk the 14km Girivalam path (though many do it barefoot).

3. Health & Protection

The tropical climate requires some preparation.

  • Sun Protection: A wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses, and high-SPF sunscreen.
  • Mosquito Repellent: Especially necessary if sitting outside during the evenings at the ashram.
  • Reusable Water Bottle: Staying hydrated is critical. Buy filtered water in bulk and refill your bottle to reduce plastic waste.
