How to Travel from Chennai to Tiruvannamalai

The complete transport guide comparing buses, trains, and private taxis for your journey.

Tiruvannamalai is located approximately 190 kilometers southwest of Chennai. It is well-connected by road and rail, making it easily accessible for domestic and international travelers arriving at Chennai International Airport (MAA). Whether you are looking for the fastest route, the most comfortable journey, or a budget-friendly option, this guide covers it all.

1. By Private Taxi / Cab (The Most Comfortable)

If you are arriving directly at Chennai Airport, hiring a private taxi is the most convenient way to reach Tiruvannamalai. It saves you the hassle of traveling into Chennai city to catch a bus or train.

  • Travel Time: 3.5 to 4 hours depending on traffic.
  • Cost: Approximately ₹3,500 to ₹5,500 depending on the vehicle type (Sedan vs SUV) and AC/Non-AC.
  • Booking: You can book pre-paid taxis directly at the airport, or use apps like Ola Outstation and Uber Intercity. Alternatively, many local travel agencies offer drop services.

2. By Bus (The Most Popular & Frequent)

Buses are the lifeline between Chennai and Tiruvannamalai. The Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation (TNSTC) operates highly frequent services.

  • Boarding Points: The primary boarding point is Kilambakkam Bus Terminus (KCBT) (recently shifted from CMBT for southbound buses).
  • Frequency: There is a bus almost every 15-30 minutes during the day.
  • Travel Time: 4 to 5 hours.
  • Cost: Very economical. Normal buses cost around ₹200-₹300. Air-conditioned (SETC) buses cost slightly more but offer better comfort.
  • Pro Tip: Try to board an "End-to-End" or "1-to-1" bus that doesn't stop at every small town along the way to save time.

3. By Train (The Most Scenic & Relaxing)

Traveling by train is comfortable and scenic, though direct options are less frequent than buses.

Currently, direct trains from Chennai (like Chennai Egmore or Tambaram) to Tiruvannamalai run on specific days, not daily. However, you can take a daily train from Chennai to Villupuram Junction (a major railway hub), and from Villupuram, take a connecting train or a 1.5-hour bus ride to Tiruvannamalai.

  • Travel Time: Total travel time is usually around 4.5 to 5 hours.
  • Cost: Depends on class (Sleeper vs AC), usually ranging from ₹150 to ₹700.
  • Booking: Ensure you book via the official IRCTC website well in advance.

Arriving in Tiruvannamalai

Once you arrive in Tiruvannamalai (either at the Central Bus Stand or the Railway Station), auto-rickshaws are readily available to take you to your accommodation. Most places around the Temple, Ramana Ashram, and the Girivalam path are within a 10-15 minute ride.
