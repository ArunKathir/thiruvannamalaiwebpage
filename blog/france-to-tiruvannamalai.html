How to Travel from France to Tiruvannamalai

Complete Transport & Travel Guide for French Travellers

Tiruvannamalai is very different from a conventional tourist destination.

It is a pilgrimage town, a spiritual centre and a place deeply connected with Arunachala, Lord Shiva, Sri Ramana Maharshi, meditation and the traditional South Indian temple culture.

For an international traveller arriving from France, the experience can be incredibly rewarding — but it is much easier when you understand how the town works before you arrive.

You may be familiar with Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Toulouse or Nice, but Tiruvannamalai operates at a very different rhythm.

You will encounter:

  • Indian temple culture
  • busy streets
  • colourful markets
  • vegetarian restaurants
  • auto-rickshaws
  • Tamil language
  • South Indian food
  • large religious gatherings
  • barefoot temple visits
  • hot and humid weather
  • spiritual communities
  • ashrams
  • long-distance train travel
  • very busy festival days

This guide is designed specifically to help French and other international travellers understand the complete journey.

France to Tiruvannamalai at a Glance

There is no airport in Tiruvannamalai.

For most international travellers arriving from France, the journey is:

France → Chennai International Airport → Tiruvannamalai

The official Tiruvannamalai district administration identifies Chennai International Airport as the nearest international airport, approximately 189 km from Tiruvannamalai. Tiruvannamalai itself has a railway station and is connected by road to major towns. Tiruvannamalai District

1. Do French Citizens Need a Visa for India?

Yes. French citizens require a visa to enter India.

France Diplomatie's current India entry guidance states that French citizens are subject to India's visa requirement and that travellers arriving without a valid visa or e-visa can be refused entry. France Diplomatie

For tourism, many French travellers can use India's e-Tourist Visa system, subject to the current eligibility and conditions.

France is currently listed among the nationalities eligible for India's e-Visa system on the official Indian government portal. Indian Visa Online

Important

Do not wait until the last moment to deal with your visa.

Before travelling, check:

  • passport validity
  • visa type
  • visa validity
  • permitted duration
  • number of entries
  • permitted activities
  • permitted arrival airports

Use the official Indian government e-Visa portal rather than an unofficial visa website.

Official India e-Visa portal

3. India's e-Arrival Card

There is another important requirement that international travellers should know.

Since 1 October 2025, foreign nationals arriving in India must complete the e-Arrival Card online.

France Diplomatie currently states that the e-Arrival Card replaces the former paper arrival form. France Diplomatie

It can be completed through the official Indian immigration system.

7. Be Careful with Separate Flight Tickets

This is particularly important for international travellers.

Suppose you book:

Paris → Dubai

and separately:

Dubai → Chennai

If the flights are on separate tickets, your baggage may not be checked through.

You could potentially need to:

  • collect luggage
  • pass immigration
  • check in again
  • go through security again

That can create visa or transit complications.

Whenever possible, understand whether your entire itinerary is issued under one booking/PNR.

France Diplomatie notes that Indian transit-visa requirements can depend on whether you leave the international transit zone, whether baggage must be collected and whether the journey is under one PNR. France Diplomatie

9. How to Travel from Chennai Airport to Tiruvannamalai

You have several choices:

Option 1 — Private taxi

Best for:

  • first-time visitors
  • couples
  • families
  • elderly travellers
  • travellers arriving late
  • travellers with large luggage

Option 2 — Prepaid airport taxi

Chennai Airport has prepaid taxi services.

The Airports Authority of India states that prepaid taxis are available from the airport's ground transportation area, with the prepaid taxi booth located in the arrivals area. Airports Authority of India

Option 3 — App-based cab

Ola and Uber operate at Chennai Airport, with designated pickup arrangements. Airports Authority of India

Option 4 — Train/bus combination

This can be cheaper but involves more planning and transfers.

For a first-time foreign visitor arriving after a long-haul flight, a pre-arranged private transfer can be considerably easier.

11. How Long Does Chennai to Tiruvannamalai Take?

The road journey is roughly 190 km, with the exact time depending heavily on traffic, departure time, route and stops.

The district administration gives approximately 189 km from Chennai Airport, while its Arunachaleswarar Temple information gives around 195 km from Chennai Central Bus Stand. Tiruvannamalai District

For an international arrival, allow extra time.

You may need time for:

  • immigration
  • baggage collection
  • customs
  • finding your driver
  • traffic
  • food/rest stops

Don't schedule your first temple visit immediately after landing.

12. Should You Take a Train from Chennai?

Yes, this is possible.

Tiruvannamalai has its own railway station.

The official district administration identifies Tiruvannamalai Railway Station as the nearest railway station for the town. Tiruvannamalai District

However, travelling from Chennai Airport to Tiruvannamalai by train generally requires more planning than taking a private car.

You may need to:

Airport → Chennai railway connection → train → Tiruvannamalai → auto/taxi → hotel

For a budget-conscious experienced traveller, this can be perfectly reasonable.

For a first-time visitor carrying two large suitcases after a long-haul flight, a private car is often logistically simpler.

17. Arunachaleswarar Temple

Your first major destination will probably be Arunachaleswarar Temple.

The temple is dedicated to Shiva and stands at the foot of Arunachala Hill. The Tiruvannamalai district administration describes it as a major Dravidian temple and gives its address in central Tiruvannamalai. Tiruvannamalai District

For a traveller from France, the architecture alone can be a remarkable experience.

Look for:

  • enormous gopurams
  • stone corridors
  • temple tanks
  • sculptures
  • shrines
  • mandapams
  • devotional activity
  • traditional rituals

But remember:

This is an active place of worship, not simply a monument.

That distinction matters.

18. Can Foreigners Visit Arunachaleswarar Temple?

International visitors do visit Tiruvannamalai and experience its temples.

However, visitors should understand that the temple is a functioning Hindu religious institution.

Respect:

  • dress requirements
  • queues
  • rituals
  • restricted areas
  • footwear rules
  • photography restrictions
  • worshippers

Do not treat religious ceremonies as entertainment.

Observe quietly and follow instructions from temple staff.

21. Photography and Mobile Phones

Do not assume that because you can photograph something in France, you can photograph it inside an Indian temple.

Photography restrictions can apply.

  • restricted areas

22. Sri Ramanasramam — One of the Biggest Attractions for Foreign Visitors

For international visitors, Sri Ramanasramam can be one of the most important reasons to visit Tiruvannamalai.

The ashram is associated with Sri Ramana Maharshi, whose teachings on self-enquiry and spiritual practice have attracted visitors from around the world.

The official ashram describes itself as a place for devotees and spiritual practice at the foothills of Arunachala. Stay Sri Ramanasramam

23. Can Foreigners Stay at Sri Ramanasramam?

This is an important distinction.

Visiting the ashram and staying at the ashram are not the same thing.

Sri Ramanasramam currently offers limited accommodation for devotees of Sri Bhagavan, including visitors from abroad, subject to availability and its registration process. The ashram states that accommodation is intended for devotees rather than general tourists, and foreign nationals can request stays subject to its rules. SRI RAMANASRAMAM

The current accommodation page says:

  • online registration is required

short-notice accommodation is not accepted

check-in is generally between 7 AM and 7 PM

  • check-out is by 1 PM

foreign nationals can stay up to 14 days under the stated policy

accommodation is subject to availability. SRI RAMANASRAMAM

Therefore, do not tell visitors:

“You can simply arrive at Ramanasramam and get a room.”

That is not how the current system works.

27. Girivalam — Walking Around Arunachala

Girivalam, also called Giri Pradakshina, is the traditional circumambulation of Arunachala.

It is one of the defining experiences of Tiruvannamalai.

The route is approximately 14 km, although the exact distance experienced by an individual can vary slightly depending on where they start and which route they take.

For an international visitor, it is important to understand that this is not simply a sightseeing walk.

For many devotees, it is a spiritual practice.

28. Should Foreigners Do Girivalam?

Yes, international visitors can participate, but prepare properly.

You should consider:

  • weather
  • walking ability
  • footwear preference
  • water
  • clothing
  • timing
  • crowd levels
  • traffic

If you are not accustomed to Indian heat, do not underestimate the walk.

A 14 km walk in hot weather can be physically demanding.

29. When Is Girivalam Especially Busy?

The full-moon day, Pournami, attracts very large numbers of devotees.

If your objective is:

  • Spiritual experience + crowd atmosphere

Pournami may be meaningful.

If your objective is:

  • Quiet exploration + meditation

A less crowded day may be more comfortable.

For international visitors, these are two completely different experiences.

31. Karthigai Deepam

Another major event is Karthigai Deepam.

The festival is deeply associated with Arunachala and Tiruvannamalai.

The temple festival can last for many days, with the major Deepam ceremony attracting huge numbers of visitors.

Tamil Nadu Tourism lists the Karthigai Deepa Brahmotsavam among the major annual festivals associated with Arunachaleswarar Temple. Tamil Nadu Tourism

book your accommodation and transportation very early.

How do I travel from France to Tiruvannamalai?

France → Chennai International Airport → Tiruvannamalai by road.

Is there a direct flight from France to Tiruvannamalai?

No. Tiruvannamalai does not have an international airport.

Fly to Chennai and continue by road or rail.

Chennai International Airport (MAA).

Do French citizens need an Indian visa?

Yes. French citizens need a valid Indian visa or e-Visa as applicable. France Diplomatie

Can French citizens apply for an Indian e-Visa?

France is currently listed among the nationalities eligible for India's e-Visa system, subject to the current eligibility rules. Indian Visa Online

Do foreigners need to complete an arrival form?

Yes. Foreign travellers arriving in India must complete the online e-Arrival Card under the current system. France Diplomatie

Can foreigners visit Arunachaleswarar Temple?

International visitors do visit Tiruvannamalai and its temple, but visitors should follow temple rules, dress respectfully and comply with restrictions on photography and access.

Can foreigners visit Sri Ramanasramam?

Yes. Foreign visitors can visit Sri Ramanasramam. Accommodation at the ashram is a separate matter and is subject to the ashram's registration and eligibility rules. SRI RAMANASRAMAM

Can foreigners stay at Sri Ramanasramam?

Foreign nationals can request accommodation, but rooms are limited and intended for devotees of Sri Bhagavan. Advance online registration is required and approval is not guaranteed. SRI RAMANASRAMAM

How long should I stay in Tiruvannamalai?

For an international visitor, 2–3 nights is a good starting point. Visitors interested deeply in spirituality, meditation or Ramana Maharshi may prefer a considerably longer stay.

Is Tiruvannamalai suitable for solo travellers?

Yes, but normal travel precautions are important. Women travelling alone should take additional care with transport and isolated areas, particularly at night. France Diplomatie provides current India-specific safety guidance. France Diplomatie

Can I do Girivalam as a foreigner?

Yes. Foreign visitors can experience Girivalam, but it is a significant walk and should be approached with appropriate preparation for heat, distance and crowd conditions.

Is Tiruvannamalai expensive for foreigners?

It can be relatively affordable compared with many international destinations, but your total cost depends on:

  • airport transfer

  • private transport
