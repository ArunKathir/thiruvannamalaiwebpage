How to Travel from Bangalore to Tiruvannamalai

Complete Bus, Train, Car & Taxi Guide

Planning a trip from Bangalore to Tiruvannamalai? Whether you're coming for Arunachaleswarar Temple, Girivalam, Pournami, Sri Ramanasramam, a spiritual retreat, or simply a peaceful weekend away from Bengaluru, getting to Tiruvannamalai is relatively straightforward.

Bengaluru and Tiruvannamalai are connected by road, bus and rail, with direct buses generally being the simplest public-transport option for many travellers. Driving is also convenient because the road journey is roughly 200–215 km depending on the route and starting point in Bengaluru. Current bus listings show multiple direct services every day, while train options are more limited and may require checking the exact service for your travel date. redBus

But the best way to travel depends on where in Bengaluru you're starting from, what time you want to arrive, whether you're travelling with family, whether you have a car, and what you plan to do after reaching Tiruvannamalai.

This guide explains all the practical options so you can choose the right one.

Why Bengaluru to Tiruvannamalai Is a Popular Weekend Journey

Tiruvannamalai is close enough to Bengaluru to make a short trip possible without taking a long holiday.

A traveller can leave Bengaluru on Friday evening, arrive in Tiruvannamalai at night, spend Saturday exploring the temple and Arunachala, and return Sunday.

However, don't assume every weekend will be equally easy.

Pournami, major temple events and Karthigai Deepam can dramatically change traffic, accommodation availability and travel times.

If your trip is centred around Girivalam or a major festival, your transport and accommodation should be planned together.

What Time Should You Leave Bangalore?

This depends on your destination and purpose.

If you want to reach Tiruvannamalai by morning

Leaving Bengaluru very early can help you avoid some city traffic and arrive with more of the day remaining.

For example:

  • 4:30 AM – Leave Bengaluru
  • Around 9:00 AM – Reach Tiruvannamalai

The actual arrival time can vary substantially, so don't treat this as a guaranteed four-and-a-half-hour journey.

If You're Travelling on Friday Evening

This is a different situation.

Friday evening traffic leaving Bengaluru can be significantly heavier than early Saturday morning traffic.

If your objective is simply: “I want to reach Tiruvannamalai as quickly as possible.” then leaving very early can be preferable to leaving during peak Friday traffic.

If you're going for a weekend stay, consider:

  • Option 1: Friday afternoon/evening → Tiruvannamalai
  • Option 2: Saturday early morning → Tiruvannamalai

Choose based on whether an extra night is worth the additional accommodation cost.

Where Should You Stop on the Way?

You don't need to stop every hour.

A practical journey could include one proper breakfast/coffee stop and one short rest or fuel stop depending on your vehicle and passengers.

Common needs include:

  • Fuel
  • Breakfast
  • Coffee
  • Toilet break
  • Child break
  • Driver rest

If you're travelling with children or elderly parents, don't optimise the trip purely for minimum driving time. A 15-minute additional break can make the entire journey considerably more comfortable.

Is the Bangalore–Tiruvannamalai Road Suitable for a Family Trip?

Yes, a private car can be particularly convenient for families because you can carry:

  • Baby supplies
  • Food
  • Extra clothes
  • Wheelchair/walking aids if required
  • Luggage
  • Water
  • Personal belongings

You can also stop whenever necessary.

But if you're travelling during Pournami or major festivals, traffic conditions near Tiruvannamalai can be very different from an ordinary weekend. Plan your parking before reaching town.

Overnight Bus vs Evening Bus

This depends on your priority.

Evening bus (You leave Bengaluru after work and arrive at night)

  • Advantages: No need to take a full day off, Wake up in Tiruvannamalai, Good for weekend trips
  • Disadvantages: Late arrival, You need confirmed accommodation, Less flexibility if the bus is delayed

Overnight bus (You travel while sleeping)

  • Advantages: Saves daytime, Can arrive early, Useful for temple visits
  • Disadvantages: Sleep quality varies, Arrival may be very early, You may still need early check-in at your accommodation

And that last point is important.

Imagine this:

Don't assume it will be free. Some properties may provide early check-in subject to availability or an additional charge.

Bangalore to Tiruvannamalai by Train

This is where things become more complicated.

Unlike buses, the railway option is not as straightforward for Bengaluru travellers. Current railway information shows limited direct service, while connecting through Katpadi is another option. eRail

One currently listed direct service is Puducherry Express, which operates between SMVT Bengaluru and Tiruvannamalai on specified days. Current timetable data lists a journey of approximately 6 hours 58 minutes for the direct service. eRail

Because railway schedules can change, always check the actual service for your travel date before making your plans.

Train via Katpadi

Another possibility is:

Bengaluru → Katpadi Junction → Tiruvannamalai

Katpadi is an important railway junction near Vellore. From Katpadi, railway services continue toward Tiruvannamalai. Current timetable data lists multiple Katpadi–Tiruvannamalai services, including daily MEMU services and other trains. eRail

This gives you another option if the direct Bengaluru service doesn't fit your schedule.

Is the Train Better Than the Bus?

For most people making a short weekend trip, the bus may be simpler because it avoids a transfer. The train becomes attractive if:

  • You enjoy train travel
  • You find a convenient direct service
  • You have a suitable connection through Katpadi
  • You want to avoid road travel
  • Your schedule aligns with the train timetable

But always compare the total journey, not just the train's travel time. If you have Bengaluru → Katpadi then waiting time + connection + Katpadi → Tiruvannamalai must all be included.

Bangalore to Tiruvannamalai by Taxi

A private taxi is the most convenient door-to-door option. You can book a Sedan, SUV, or Larger vehicle for groups. The main advantage isn't necessarily speed. It's convenience. A taxi can pick you up from your home, apartment, hotel or office in Bengaluru and take you directly to your accommodation in Tiruvannamalai.

When Does a Taxi Make Sense?

A private taxi can be useful for:

  • Families: You don't need to manage multiple bus seats.
  • Elderly travellers: Door-to-door travel can reduce walking and transfers.
  • Groups: A larger vehicle can sometimes make economic sense when the cost is divided among several people.
  • Temple-focused trips: You can carry luggage and travel directly to your accommodation.
  • Short trips: If you're trying to maximise a one- or two-day trip, eliminating transfers can be valuable.

One-Way Taxi vs Round Trip

Before booking a taxi, compare One-way fare versus Round-trip package. If you're returning to Bengaluru after two days, a round-trip arrangement may be worth considering. Ask the driver/company:

  • Is toll included?
  • Is parking included?
  • Is driver allowance included?
  • Is interstate tax included?
  • Is waiting time included?
  • Is the return trip fixed? What happens if you return late?

Don't compare only the headline price. Compare the final payable amount.

Bangalore to Tiruvannamalai for Pournami

If you're coming specifically for Pournami Girivalam, don't plan your trip like an ordinary weekend. The crowd can be dramatically larger. Your priorities should become:

  1. Room: Confirm accommodation first.
  2. Transport: Decide how you're getting there.
  3. Parking: If driving, understand where you can park.
  4. Girivalam: Plan when you'll start.
  5. Return journey: Don't book a very tightly timed return immediately after the walk.

Should You Drive During Pournami?

You can drive to Tiruvannamalai, but the question isn't simply whether you can reach the town. The question is: Where will you park once you arrive?

Crowd-management measures can change during major pilgrimage periods. So if you're coming from Bengaluru for Pournami, confirm the current traffic and parking arrangements close to your travel date.

Bangalore to Tiruvannamalai During Karthigai Deepam

Karthigai Deepam is an entirely different travel situation. The number of visitors can be enormous, and accommodation and transport demand can rise significantly.

Your priorities should be: Book room → confirm location → confirm arrival route → understand parking/traffic → plan walking.

Do not leave accommodation until the last minute. TiruvannamalaiRooms.com itself currently warns that Deepam accommodation can sell out well in advance. Tiruvannamalai Rooms

What to Do Immediately After Reaching Tiruvannamalai

If you arrive by bus, you will likely need to get from the bus drop point to your accommodation. Before travelling, ask your accommodation provider: “Which bus stop should I get down at?”

This is particularly important because buses can have different drop points. Current bus listings show common drop locations including the Tiruvannamalai bus stand and areas around Ramana Ashram. redBus

Getting Around Tiruvannamalai After Arrival

Once you arrive, local transport is relatively straightforward. TiruvannamalaiRooms.com's local transport guide describes share-autos as a common local transport option and also discusses private autos and walking between major areas. Tiruvannamalai Rooms

Depending on where your room is, you may use: Share-auto, Private auto-rickshaw, Your own car, Taxi, Walking, or Two-wheeler. If your room is close to your main destinations, walking can be convenient.

Where Should Bangalore Travellers Stay?

This is particularly important. Don't automatically book the closest room to the temple. Choose according to your itinerary.

  • If You're Coming for Arunachaleswarar Temple: Consider rooms near the temple. Advantages: Convenient access, Less dependence on local transport, Easy return to your room, Useful for early/late temple visits.
  • If You're Coming for Ramanasramam: Consider accommodation around Ramana Ashram / Chengam Road. This can reduce your daily travel if the ashram is your primary destination.
  • If You're Coming for Girivalam: Consider accommodation with convenient access to the Girivalam route. Your room doesn't necessarily need to be directly beside the route. The important thing is that you can reach your starting point easily.
  • If You're Travelling With Family: Prioritise AC, Family room, Parking, Hot water, Bathroom quality, Lift/ground floor, Quietness. Location comes after your essential requirements.

Why Bangalore Travellers Shouldn't Choose a Room Only by Price

Imagine your bus arrives at 5 AM. Your room is 4 km away. The property doesn't offer early check-in. There is no nearby restaurant. And you have luggage.

A room that saved you ₹300 suddenly isn't necessarily the cheapest option in terms of overall convenience. Instead, look at: Room price + transport + convenience + time + comfort. That's the real cost of accommodation.

Common Mistakes Bangalore Travellers Make

  • Mistake 1: Leaving Bengaluru too late on Friday. Traffic can make the journey much longer than expected.
  • Mistake 2: Booking the room after starting the journey. Especially risky during Pournami and festivals.
  • Mistake 3: Assuming the hotel is walking distance. Check the actual distance.
  • Mistake 4: Forgetting early check-in. An overnight bus may arrive hours before standard hotel check-in.
  • Mistake 5: Planning Girivalam immediately after a long drive. You may be exhausted before you even start.
  • Mistake 6: Driving directly into crowded areas during major events. Check local traffic and parking arrangements.
  • Mistake 7: Booking the cheapest room without checking facilities. A slightly better room can make a major difference after a long journey.
