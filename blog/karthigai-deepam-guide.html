The Ultimate Guide to Karthigai Deepam

How to experience the grand festival of lights in Tiruvannamalai safely and comfortably.

Karthigai Deepam is the crown jewel of Tiruvannamalai's spiritual calendar. Falling in the Tamil month of Karthigai (usually Nov/Dec), the festival culminates in the lighting of a massive cauldron of ghee at the very peak of Arunachala Hill—a flame that burns for days and can be seen for miles.

What to Expect

The town transforms during the 10-day festival. Over a million pilgrims descend upon Tiruvannamalai. The streets are packed, the energy is electric, and devotion is visible everywhere.

  • The Bharani Deepam: Lit inside the temple in the early morning.
  • The Maha Deepam: Lit atop the hill exactly at sunset (around 6 PM). The moment it catches fire, the entire town erupts in chants of "Arunachala Shiva!"

Booking Accommodation (Crucial!)

Rooms for the main days of Karthigai Deepam sell out months in advance. Prices skyrocket due to demand. If you plan to attend, you must book your hotel at least 3-4 months early.

Because traffic is completely locked down by the police, you should try to book a room within walking distance of the Girivalam path or the main temple so you aren't reliant on transport.

Survival Tips

  • Walk Everywhere: Autos and cars are mostly banned from entering the town center during the peak days.
  • Stay Hydrated: The crowds can make it claustrophobic. Carry a bottle of water.
  • Watch Your Belongings: Pickpockets operate in dense crowds. Keep your phone and wallet secure.
