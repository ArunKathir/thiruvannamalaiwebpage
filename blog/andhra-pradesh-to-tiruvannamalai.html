How to Travel from Andhra Pradesh to Tiruvannamalai

Complete Bus, Train, Car & Flight Guide.

Planning a trip from Andhra Pradesh to Tiruvannamalai? Whether you are travelling from Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Nellore, Tirupati, Chittoor or another part of Andhra Pradesh, this complete travel guide explains the different ways to reach Tiruvannamalai by bus, train, car, taxi and flight.

Tiruvannamalai is one of Tamil Nadu's most important pilgrimage destinations and is particularly known for Arunachaleswarar Temple, Arunachala Hill, Girivalam, Sri Ramana Ashram and Pournami pilgrimage days.

For travellers coming from Andhra Pradesh, the journey can vary significantly depending on where you start. Someone travelling from Chittoor or Tirupati has a very different journey from someone travelling from Vijayawada or Visakhapatnam.

The good news is that Tiruvannamalai has a railway station and good road connectivity. The official Tiruvannamalai district website confirms that Tiruvannamalai is connected by road with major towns and that the town has its own railway station. Chennai is the nearest major international airport, approximately 189 km away according to the district administration. Tiruvannamalai District

This guide explains the journey step by step so you can choose the route that fits your budget, travel time, family requirements and trip purpose.

2. How Far Is Andhra Pradesh from Tiruvannamalai?

There is no single answer because the distance depends on your starting city.

For example, the road distance from Vijayawada to Tiruvannamalai is roughly in the mid-500-kilometre range, depending on the route used. Current route-planning data places the drive at roughly 538–566 km and around 8–9 hours before accounting for substantial breaks or traffic. Rome2Rio

From Nellore, the road distance is around 306 km, while the railway journey can be considerably longer because rail routes do not simply follow the road. Rome2Rio

From Visakhapatnam, the road journey is around 900 km, making train or flight-based options more attractive for many travellers. Rome2Rio

    • 3. Andhra Pradesh to Tiruvannamalai by Train

For many travellers, train is one of the most practical options, especially when travelling a long distance from Andhra Pradesh.

Tiruvannamalai has its own railway station, so if your train reaches Tiruvannamalai Railway Station (TNM), you do not need to travel to another major city and then take a long road journey.

The official Tiruvannamalai administration confirms that the town is served by rail and that Tiruvannamalai railway station is the town's railway station. Tiruvannamalai District

However, train availability varies considerably depending on your starting city.

Tirupati is another important transport hub for Andhra Pradesh travellers.

Current railway listings show multiple services from Tirupati/Renigunta toward Tiruvannamalai, with some services taking approximately 4–6 hours depending on the train and boarding station. eRail

For someone already visiting Tirupati, this creates an interesting travel combination:

Tirupati → Tiruvannamalai

You can visit Tirupati first and then continue your spiritual journey toward Tiruvannamalai.

5. Nellore to Tiruvannamalai by Train

Nellore is another important Andhra Pradesh city with rail connectivity toward Tiruvannamalai.

Current railway listings show several services between Nellore (NLR) and Tiruvannamalai, with journey times varying depending on the service. One listed service takes approximately 6 hours 46 minutes, while other services take longer. eRail

Route-planning information also currently shows direct train options from Nellore to Tiruvannamalai on certain days. Rome2Rio

8. Andhra Pradesh to Tiruvannamalai by Bus

Bus travel is another major option.

However, the availability of a single direct bus depends heavily on your starting city.

A traveller from Chittoor or Tirupati has a much easier road connection to Tiruvannamalai than someone travelling from Visakhapatnam.

For some long-distance journeys, you may need to change buses at a major transport point.

  • Possible combinations include:

    • Andhra city → Tirupati → Tiruvannamalai

  • or

    • Andhra city → Chittoor → Tiruvannamalai

The exact route depends on the buses available on your travel date.

9. Tirupati to Tiruvannamalai by Bus

For travellers starting from Tirupati, bus travel can be a straightforward option.

The route is significantly shorter than travelling from Vijayawada or Visakhapatnam, and Tirupati is a major transport hub.

26. Travelling from Andhra Pradesh During Pournami

Pournami is one of the busiest periods for Tiruvannamalai because many devotees visit for Girivalam around Arunachala Hill.

If your trip is planned around Pournami, don't treat it like a normal weekend.

Accommodation demand can change significantly.

  • Plan:

    • Transport → Room → Temple/Girivalam → Return journey

rather than booking transportation first and searching for a room after reaching Tiruvannamalai.

This is especially important if you are travelling from a distant Andhra city.

27. Andhra Pradesh to Tiruvannamalai During Karthigai Deepam

Karthigai Deepam is another period when Tiruvannamalai experiences exceptionally high visitor demand.

If you are travelling from Andhra Pradesh during Deepam season, plan well in advance.

  • Consider:
  • train tickets

  • parking
  • local transportation
  • return journey

Do not assume you can arrive and find accommodation easily during the busiest dates.

For a festival trip, transport and accommodation should be treated as one booking plan.

Technically, yes for travellers from closer locations.

But whether it is enjoyable depends on your starting point.
