A Day Trip to Sathanur Dam

Escape to nature with a short drive from Tiruvannamalai.

A Nature Escape

If you're staying in Tiruvannamalai for a few days and want a break from the spiritual rigor to enjoy some nature, a half-day trip to Sathanur Dam is the perfect choice. Located just 30 kilometers (about a 45-minute drive) from Tiruvannamalai, it offers a refreshing change of scenery.

About Sathanur Dam

Constructed across the Thenpennai River in the 1950s, Sathanur Dam is an engineering marvel and a major water source for the region. Surrounded by the beautiful Chennakesava Hills, the reservoir creates a massive, picturesque lake.

What to See and Do

  • The Dam and Reservoir View: The view of the vast water body against the backdrop of rolling hills is stunning, particularly during the monsoon and post-monsoon months when the water levels are high.
  • The Crocodile Farm: The dam houses a dedicated crocodile conservation park, which is quite popular with kids and families.
  • Beautiful Gardens: Similar to the famous Brindavan Gardens, Sathanur features beautifully manicured parks with fountains. It's a great spot for a family picnic.

Travel Tips

Timing: The best time to visit is early morning or late afternoon to avoid the midday sun.

Transport: You can easily hire a taxi from Tiruvannamalai for a round trip. Local buses also run towards the dam, though a private vehicle is more convenient.
