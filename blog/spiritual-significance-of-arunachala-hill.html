The Spiritual Significance of Arunachala Hill

Why a solitary mountain in Tamil Nadu is considered the center of the universe for spiritual seekers.

A Mountain That Is God

In most Hindu traditions, a mountain may be considered the abode of a deity—Mount Kailash is where Shiva lives, and Tirumala is where Vishnu resides. However, Arunachala Hill in Tiruvannamalai holds a unique status: the mountain is not considered the abode of Shiva; it is worshipped as Lord Shiva Himself manifesting in the form of a physical mountain.

The Mythology of the Endless Column of Light

According to the Puranas, a cosmic dispute once arose between Lord Brahma (the creator) and Lord Vishnu (the preserver) over who was supreme. To settle the dispute, Lord Shiva appeared before them as an infinite, blazing column of fire (Jyotirlinga) that stretched from the depths of the netherworld to the highest heavens.

Shiva challenged them: whoever could find the end of the fiery column would be declared supreme. Vishnu took the form of a boar (Varaha) and dug deep to find the base, while Brahma took the form of a swan and flew high to find the crown. Both failed, humbling their egos and recognizing Shiva's supremacy.

At the request of the gods, Shiva cooled his fiery form into a physical mountain so that devotees could worship him in a tangible form. That mountain is Arunachala.

The Magnet of Sri Ramana Maharshi

While the mythological significance has drawn devotees for millennia, the global renown of Arunachala in modern times is inextricably linked to the great sage Sri Ramana Maharshi. Leaving his home as a teenager, he was inexplicably pulled to Arunachala and never left its base for the remaining 54 years of his life.

He famously said, "Kailash is the abode of Shiva, but Arunachala is Shiva himself."

The Power of Girivalam

Because the hill is the deity, circumambulating the hill (Girivalam) is the highest form of worship here. Millions walk the 14km path, especially during the full moon, absorbing the immense, silent, transformative energy radiating from the mountain.
