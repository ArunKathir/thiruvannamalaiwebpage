Connecting with God Through Girivalam

The inner spiritual journey of walking the 14km path.

Millions of people walk the 14-kilometer path around Mount Arunachala every full moon. But Girivalam is much more than a physical trek; it is a moving meditation designed to break down the ego and connect the soul directly with God.

Walking Barefoot

The practice is traditionally done barefoot. This is not just an ascetic penance; it connects your physical body directly to the sacred earth of the mountain. Every step is considered to be on holy ground, grounding the mind and humbling the ego.

The Moving Meditation

As you walk, the shape of Arunachala constantly changes. Sometimes it looks like a single peak, sometimes like five faces (the Panchamukha darshan). This shifting perspective is meant to remind the seeker that God can be viewed in infinite ways, yet remains the singular truth at the center.

How to Connect Deeply

To truly connect with God during Girivalam, do not treat it as a social event. Walk in silence. Chant the mantra "Om Namah Shivaya" or "Arunachala Shiva" internally with every step. By the time you complete the 14km circle, you will find that the physical exhaustion has quieted the mind, leaving only a profound sense of inner peace.
