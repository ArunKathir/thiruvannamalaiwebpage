The Grandeur of Karthigai Deepam

Witnessing a million lamps and the blazing Maha Deepam atop the holy hill.

What is Karthigai Deepam?

Karthigai Deepam is arguably the most spectacular and spiritually charged festival celebrated in Tiruvannamalai. Occurring during the full moon of the Tamil month of Karthigai (usually falling in November or December), it is a festival of lights that commemorates the manifestation of Lord Shiva as an infinite column of fire.

The Lighting of the Maha Deepam

The climax of the 10-day festival occurs on the final evening. As the sun sets, a massive copper cauldron containing nearly 3,500 kilograms of ghee and a 1,000-meter-long cotton wick is carried to the very summit of Arunachala Hill.

Precisely at 6:00 PM, simultaneous with the lighting of the five deepams inside the Arunachaleswarar Temple, the Maha Deepam (the great light) is lit atop the mountain. The collective roar of "Annamalaiyarukku Arohara!" from millions of devotees standing at the base echoes through the town. The flame burns continuously for the next 11 days, visible for miles around.

The 10-Day Brahmotsavam

The days leading up to the Maha Deepam are filled with incredible fervor. The temple deities are taken out in grand processions on elaborate vahanas (chariots). The wooden temple chariots, pulled by thousands of devotees, are a magnificent sight.

Tips for Visiting During Karthigai Deepam

  • Crowds: Expect upwards of 3 to 4 million people to visit Tiruvannamalai on the day of the Deepam.
  • Book Early: Accommodation sells out months in advance. You absolutely must book your stay as early as possible.
  • Transportation: Private vehicles are usually stopped at the outskirts of the town on the main festival days. Prepare to walk significantly.
