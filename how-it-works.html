How Tiruvannamalai Rooms Works

A simple, transparent booking process powered by personal service.

We Are a Professional Booking Agency

With over 20 years of experience, Tiruvannamalai Rooms provides a professional and private accommodation booking service exclusively for International and Indian customers. We connect visitors with quality accommodation in Tiruvannamalai. We do not own or operate any hotels. Instead, we maintain a curated network of properties that we have personally assessed, and we recommend suitable options based on your specific needs.

The Process

Step 1: Tell Us What You Need

Use our Find My Room form or simply WhatsApp us. Share the following:

  • Your check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests (adults and children)
  • Your budget per night
  • Preferred location near Arunachaleswarar Temple, Ramana Ashram, Girivalam route, bus stand, or railway station
  • Stay type premium, family, couple, or budget
  • Specific requirements AC, parking, Wi-Fi, hot water, lift, family-friendly, etc.
  • Any other preferences quiet room, early check-in, elderly-friendly, ground floor, etc.

Step 2: We Search and Recommend

Our team reviews your requirements and searches through our network of verified properties in Tiruvannamalai. We look for options that match your budget, location preference, and special needs. We typically respond within a few hours via WhatsApp with our recommendations.

Step 3: You Review and Decide

We share details about the recommended properties including room type, amenities, location specifics, and rates. You can ask questions, request alternatives, or adjust your criteria. There is no pressure to book.

Step 4: Book and Stay

Once you choose a property, we help facilitate the booking directly with the accommodation. You stay at the property and pay the rate set by the property. We earn a commission from the property there is no extra charge to you.

What Makes This Different?

  • Personal service You talk to a real person, not a search algorithm.
  • Local knowledge We know Tiruvannamalai. We know which areas suit which types of visitors.
  • Curated selection We do not list every property. We recommend only stays we believe are worth recommending.
  • Verified properties We visit properties in person. We check room conditions, cleanliness, amenities, and safety.
  • No account needed No sign-up, no app download, no complicated dashboard. Just WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked

Is there any extra charge for using your service?

No. You pay the room rate set by the property. We earn our commission from the property, not from you.

How quickly do you respond?

We aim to respond to all WhatsApp enquiries within a few hours. During peak seasons (festivals, full moon days), response times may be slightly longer.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Simply provide the guest's details along with your enquiry.
